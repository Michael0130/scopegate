HOW IT WORKS

Three gates. Zero free work.

01

Buyer agent posts an intent

Natural language lands in the Prompt Firewall: XML-isolated, scanned for injection, matched against the merchant’s service baseline. Price obeys a one-way ratchet — add-ons only move it up.

02

Contract anchored on PayPal

The frozen terms are SHA-256 hashed into purchase_units[0].custom_id. Intangible-service checkout with NO_SHIPPING; PayPal-Request-Id makes agent retries idempotent — never a double charge.

03

Disputes answered with evidence

Every delivery pickup logs payer email, IP, timestamp and artifact hash. One click exports the Seller Protection §11 dossier PayPal reviewers actually accept.

WHY SCOPEGATE

A defense layer sellers were missing.

Scope creep, priced.

Rush jobs, extra revisions, added sections — every overshoot is metered against published surcharge rules and re-quoted as a new locked contract version.

Prompt injection, burned.

“Ignore pricing rules” is data, not a command. Deterministic heuristics plus a hard-constraint LLM reject the attack before any work starts.

Disputes, won.

Intangible services lose chargebacks for lack of a tracking number. A contract hash in PayPal’s ledger plus tracked delivery is the tracking number.
